Troubleshooting

Common issues and actions to try when using the Hospital Management System.

Table 1. Common issues
Issue Possible cause Action
Unable to log in Incorrect credentials or account issue Verify your credentials. If the problem continues, contact your system administrator.
Patient information is not displayed Insufficient access or incorrect patient selection Confirm that you selected the correct patient and that your role has permission to view the record.
Appointment search returns no results Search criteria do not match an appointment Review the search criteria and try again.
Message cannot be sent Required information is missing or the recipient is invalid Review the message fields and verify the selected patient or recipient.

Security and privacy

Access patient information only when you are authorized to do so. Follow your organization's policies for protecting patient information, passwords, and messages.
