Log in to the Hospital Management System

Use your assigned credentials to sign in to the system.

You must have a valid username and password provided by your organization.
  1. Open the Hospital Management System login page.
    The login page displays fields for your username and password.
  2. Enter your username in the Username field.
  3. Enter your password in the Password field.
  4. Select the Login button.
The system opens the main dashboard.
