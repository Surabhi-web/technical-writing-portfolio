View Patient Information

Open a patient's profile to review the information available to your role.

Make sure you are authorized to access the patient's information.
  1. Open the patient record from the available patient-management area.
  2. Select the patient whose information you need to review.
  3. Review the information displayed on the patient profile.
The selected patient's profile information is displayed.
Close or leave the patient record when you finish reviewing the information, according to your organization's privacy procedures.
